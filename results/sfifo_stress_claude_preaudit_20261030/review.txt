# Pre-outcome methods review: `heldout_sfifo_stress` (synthetic redundancy variant of the ZipCPU `sfifo`)

**Recommendation: no-go for the pilot as currently specified.** It can become a go once the blockers in section 4 are fixed. The base setup is sound: the checker, the labelling, and the equivalence proof all hold up. The problems are in the primary target definition, how the B trial is being used, and one P-arm boundary I couldn't check.

I read all twelve files, including all 1193 lines of the equivalence log. No files were edited, nothing was run, and A, P and C outcomes were neither created nor assumed.

## 2. Feasibility of the primary target (finding 6)

This is a lower-bound argument only. No stimulus vectors are given.

**Starting state.** In the seed, lines 90 and 95 (the pure-read and pure-write case arms) have 0 hits. So the seed accepts no reads or writes and leaves the FIFO empty with both pointers at 0.

**Write event (lines 96/179).** This needs an accepted pure write while fill is 15. That takes at least 16 cycles that are pure writes.

**Wrap-read event (lines 91/161).** This needs an accepted pure read while `rd_addr[3:0]==15`. That takes at least 16 cycles that advance `rd_addr`, with the last one being a pure read. A simultaneous read and write while not full falls under case `2'b11`, which doesn't hit this branch.

**Lower bound.** A cycle that advances `rd_addr` can't also be a pure write, so the two sets of cycles don't overlap. That gives **≥32 generated cycles** with no resets.

**Against the budget.** Five rounds of 8 cycles give 40 cycles, so the earliest possible success is round 4 with zero slack, and round 5 allows 8 cycles of slack. Calibration shows that 32 cycles are enough.

**What the target would measure:**
- Feedback can't show progress on these counters until about round 4. A model that emits a canonical fill-and-drain early will pass in every arm. One that builds up step by step from feedback will probably fail in every arm. So the outcome measures planning, not use of feedback.
- With n small, expect floor or ceiling effects and very little power.
- The ≥20/24 condition is close to implied by the late events, given the 22/24 calibration ceiling.
- If rounds restart from reset (`require_initial_reset` applied per round), the target can't be reached at all. I have not verified how rounds are composed.

## 4. Recommendation: no-go as specified; conditional go after these fixes

**Blockers:**
1. **P source.** Confirm in code that P's prompt is built from P's own trajectory, never from the C arm's live prompt.
2. **Target definition.**
   - Pre-register a target that doesn't use the B development plateau.
   - Define success as the two events (write at fill 15, wrap read), with line 129 either included or its exclusion justified.
   - Drop line coverage as an outcome.
   - Fix the cycle budget from the 32-cycle bound, for example with more rounds or a per-event secondary endpoint.
3. **Round composition.** Verify that state carries over between rounds and that each round isn't reset.
4. **Capacity.** Confirm the stress harness calls `check(..., capacity=16)` and fix the stale docstring.

**Non-blocking (fix and disclose):**
- Remove the e-graph wording and the "near-full/pointer-wrap" hint from the spec shown to the model, or document them as shared hints.
- Remove C's calibration-based reachability filter, or apply the same filter to B.
- Restate the equivalence scope as `equiv_simple` with register correspondence, two-state, fixed parameters.
- Report the candidate-screening count.
- Keep the B trial out of any formal analysis.
- Always call this a disclosed synthetic variant of the ZipCPU FIFO, never a natural second benchmark IP.
